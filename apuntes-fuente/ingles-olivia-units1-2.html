Unit 1 · It's… They're…

✔️ Sí❌ No

VariosThey're noisy. (They're = They are)They aren't quiet. (They aren't = They are not)

  • It's a parrot. It's colourful. It isn't quiet. = Es un loro. Es de colores. No es silencioso.
  • They're dolphins. They're big. They aren't small. = Son delfines. Son grandes. No son pequeños.
  • It's green. It's got four legs. → It's a frog. = Es verde. Tiene cuatro patas. → Es una rana.
  • What is it? = ¿Qué es? · Are they big? Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
  • Are you afraid of spiders? Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. · I'm afraid of sharks. = Me dan miedo los tiburones.

Unit 2 · This is my… These are our…

Una cosaVarias cosas
This is my speaker. = Este es mi altavoz.These are our lights. = Estas son nuestras luces.
This is my alarm clock.These are my photos.

my = mi (de una persona) · our = nuestro, nuestra (de varios) · This = esto, este, esta · These = estos, estas.

  • Have you got a rug in your bedroom? = ¿Tienes una alfombra en tu habitación? → Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
  • What's your favourite thing in your bedroom? = ¿Cuál es tu cosa favorita de tu habitación?
  • This isn't a cushion. It's a cat! = ¡Esto no es un cojín! ¡Es un gato!
